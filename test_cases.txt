CPU SCHEDULING SIMULATOR - TEST CASES
Ganashree C.A.

These test cases are used to verify the FCFS scheduling algorithm.


==================================================
TEST CASE 1 - NORMAL CASE
==================================================

Number of processes: 5

PID    Arrival    Burst    Priority
P1     0          5        2
P2     1          3        1
P3     2          8        3
P4     4          2        2
P5     6          4        1

Time Quantum: 2

Expected FCFS Gantt Chart:

P1: 0 - 5
P2: 5 - 8
P3: 8 - 16
P4: 16 - 18
P5: 18 - 22

Expected FCFS Results:

PID    Completion    Turnaround    Waiting
P1     5             5             0
P2     8             7             4
P3     16            14            6
P4     18            14            12
P5     22            16            12

Average Waiting Time = 6.8
Average Turnaround Time = 11.2


==================================================
TEST CASE 2 - SAME ARRIVAL TIME AND TIE-BREAKING
==================================================

Number of processes: 5

PID    Arrival    Burst    Priority
P1     0          4        2
P2     0          4        1
P3     0          2        3
P4     0          2        2
P5     0          6        1

Time Quantum: 2

FCFS tie-breaking:
If arrival times are equal, use the lower PID.

Expected FCFS Gantt Chart:

P1: 0 - 4
P2: 4 - 8
P3: 8 - 10
P4: 10 - 12
P5: 12 - 18

Expected FCFS Results:

PID    Completion    Turnaround    Waiting
P1     4             4             0
P2     8             8             4
P3     10            10            8
P4     12            12            10
P5     18            18            12

Average Waiting Time = 6.8
Average Turnaround Time = 10.4


==================================================
TEST CASE 3 - INITIAL CPU IDLE TIME
==================================================

Number of processes: 4

PID    Arrival    Burst    Priority
P1     2          3        2
P2     4          5        1
P3     5          2        3
P4     7          4        2

Time Quantum: 2

Expected FCFS Gantt Chart:

Idle: 0 - 2
P1:   2 - 5
P2:   5 - 10
P3:   10 - 12
P4:   12 - 16

Expected FCFS Results:

PID    Completion    Turnaround    Waiting
P1     5             3             0
P2     10            6             1
P3     12            7             5
P4     16            9             5

Average Waiting Time = 2.75
Average Turnaround Time = 6.25


==================================================
FORMULAS
==================================================

Turnaround Time = Completion Time - Arrival Time

Waiting Time = Turnaround Time - Burst Time


==================================================
PURPOSE OF THE TEST CASES
==================================================

Test Case 1:
Checks normal FCFS scheduling with mixed arrival times.

Test Case 2:
Checks equal arrival times, equal burst times and PID
tie-breaking.

Test Case 3:
Checks CPU idle time before the first process arrives.
